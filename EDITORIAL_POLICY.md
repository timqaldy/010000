# EDITORIAL_POLICY

Permanent publication rules. Thresholds belong in configuration, not in this file.

## Constitution

1. Every factual claim requires evidence.
2. Separate facts from assumptions.
3. No unsupported causal claims.
4. No clickbait by default.
5. Do not exaggerate uncertainty, danger, or scale.
6. Do not optimize headlines for fear.
7. Do not call a person guilty without an appropriate factual/legal basis.
8. Protect unnecessary personal information.
9. Do not publish private contact details.
10. Do not expose private residential addresses unless necessary and legally appropriate.
11. If reliable sources conflict, show the conflict.
12. Never silently pick the convenient source.
13. Corrections must remain visible.
14. Original source attribution must be accessible.
15. Utility beats engagement bait.

## Safety check

Before autonomous publication evaluate: unsupported claims, source mismatch, defamation, PII, sensationalism, date/location mismatch, hallucinated details, source contradiction, copyright risk, restricted source usage.

Outcome: `PASS` | `FAIL` | `REVIEW`.

- PASS → may continue  
- FAIL → block  
- REVIEW → hold from publication (not “invent a human editor in MVP”)

## Auto-publish

Publish only if all are true: required evidence; source allowed; confidence threshold; importance threshold; safety = PASS; no blocking contradiction; required attribution available.
